# I Pee Infringement: The Game

A little browser shooter. Blast crates, drones and silly bosses, grab coins, bank them at home, and gear up your bot.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages link for this repo (Settings → Pages), or just open `index.html` in a browser.

Controls: WASD move, mouse aim, click or space shoot, E at your house to customize, Esc to end the round.
Progress (coins, gear, high scores) saves in your own browser.
